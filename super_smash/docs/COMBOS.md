# Reward v2: combos, spam and defence

Reward used to train MK3 and MK4 (`league.py`, `SmashEnv(reward="v2")`). The code is
`smash_rl/rewards.py` (all terms) and `smash_rl/combos.py` (combo lists and tracker).
This page is generated by `docs/make_combos_doc.py`; rerun it after changing either file.

## Why it changed

MK1 / MK2 (reward v1 plus a whiff penalty on A attacks only) learned to camp: Fox fired
lasers all game (specials were never penalised, and lasers deal damage without being punished)
while the other agent stood still or kept dodging, because taking no damage scores better than
risking an approach. v2 makes knockouts the dominant reward, makes every attack that misses
cost something, punishes using one move over and over, and punishes going a long time without
hurting anyone.

## Terms (per player, per frame)

| Term | Weight | When |
|---|---|---|
| Knockout | +2.0 / -2.0 | Opponent loses a stock / you lose one (the largest term by far) |
| Damage | ±0.003 per % | Damage dealt minus damage taken |
| Combo step (listed combo) | +0.1 x step, from step 2 | Step k >= 2 of a combo from the table below, if it connects and chains (step 1 pays like any hit) |
| Chain hit (any true combo) | +0.03 x (hits - 1), at most 5 steps | A hit that lands while the opponent is still stunned, not part of a listed combo |
| Single hit | +0.003 | A hit that starts or continues nothing, and every pummel |
| Whiff | -0.03 (smash attack: -0.2) | An attack (A move or special) ends without touching the opponent or their shield |
| **Smash spam** | -0.2 x 2^(n-2), up to -2.0 | The n-th forward / up / down smash started within 240 frames (4 s), n >= 2: -0.2, -0.4, -0.8, -1.6, -2.0 |
| **Move spam** | -0.1 x 2^(extra-1), up to -2.0 | A move used more than 3 times in your last 6 actions (exceptions below): -0.1, -0.2, -0.4 ... |
| Passive | -0.007 per second x opponents alive | No damage dealt for more than 120 frames (2 s); Fox and Falco laser hits don't reset this timer |
| **Defence spam** | -0.1 x 2^(extra-1), up to -2.0 | A shield, roll, spot dodge or air dodge starts while more than 3 of your last 6 actions were defences |
| Shield held | -0.05 per second | Shield up for more than 60 frames (1 s) in a row |

Every spam penalty doubles with each extra repetition and is capped at one knockout, so
sustained spam costs more than losing stocks. Exceptions (moves that are normal to use a lot):

- FOX neutral_b: penalised only past 6 uses in the last 7 attacks.

Shielding and dodging earn nothing by themselves: the agent has to discover that they
save it from damage and knockouts. (Earlier versions paid for blocks and dodges, and agents
learned to farm those rewards instead of fighting.) Overusing them still costs a little.

## How attacks and hits are detected

- **Moves** come from the attacker's action state: jabs, tilts, smashes, aerials, pummel and the
  four throws map to fixed names. Character-specific action states (id >= 0x155) count as specials
  only if B was pressed in the last 10 frames (they also cover extra jumps, floats, etc.);
  which one (`neutral_b`, `side_b`, `up_b`, `down_b`) comes from the direction of that B input.
  Up-B and side-B started off stage are recovery moves and never count as whiffs.
- **Connects** means the opponent lost percent or a stock while the attack was out. Shield hits
  count as connecting for the whiff rule but give no combo reward. A special also counts if the
  opponent is hurt within 40 frames after it ends (lasers and other projectiles).
  Multi-hit moves count once per use.
- **True combo (chain):** SmashWiki defines a true combo as a sequence of attacks that keeps the
  opponent in hitstun; Melee's combo counter tolerates one frame of non-hitstun. Here the opponent
  counts as stunned while in a damage, tumble, thrown or grabbed action state, and a hit chains if
  the opponent has been out of those states for at most 15 frames (more lenient than the
  game, so chain throws and regrabs count) and the previous hit was at most 120 frames ago.
- **Listed combo:** each character has combo sequences (table below). A hit that continues a combo
  the attacker has been doing (chained, and the right next move) moves it one step forward and pays
  `listed_step x step` from step 2 (0.2, 0.3, ...); a hit with a combo's first move starts it at
  step 1, which pays like any other hit, so only continuing a combo is worth more.
  A whiff, or a hit that doesn't chain, resets it. If no listed combo advances, a chained hit pays
  the chain reward and anything else pays the single-hit reward.
